# Unit 1: Purpose, Scope, and Literature Review

## Purpose and scope

The purpose of this project is to help employees recognize phishing attempts and make
safer decisions when dealing with suspicious emails, links, websites, or requests for
sensitive information. Employees are often the first target attackers try to take
advantage of.

The scope includes researching common phishing methods, reviewing current awareness
practices, creating training materials, and finding a way to measure whether employees
understand the training. It also includes recommendations for how the training can be
repeated or improved over time.

The project does not include real phishing attacks, confidential company information, or
changes to an organization's existing security systems. Keeping the project within these
limits lets it focus on employee awareness and behavior.

## Literature review

A literature review shows what is already known about a problem and where gaps still
need more research (Sheppard, 2019).

**Technical detection.** Subhashini et al. (2024) built a system that analyzes website
links for signs that they may be fraudulent. Their model identified phishing websites
with 98.89% accuracy, which shows technical tools can be very effective at detection.

**Research coverage.** Althobaiti and Alsufyani (2024) reviewed 163 organization-focused
phishing research articles and found that most existing research focuses on detection
and user awareness, while phishing mitigation receives less attention.

**Training effectiveness.** Ho et al. (2025) studied roughly 19,500 employees over eight
months and found no significant relationship between annual training completion and
phishing susceptibility. Lain et al. (2024) found that a plain reminder performed about
as well as full training content.

## Problem sketch

Employees can still be vulnerable to phishing even when technical detection tools and
basic awareness programs are already in place. This creates a need for practical training
that improves how employees recognize and respond to phishing attempts over time, and for
a way to measure whether that improvement actually happened.
