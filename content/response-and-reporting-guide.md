# Response and Reporting Guide

## Step 1. Stop
Do not click links, open attachments, reply, or forward the message to a coworker
to ask about it. Forwarding spreads the message.

## Step 2. Verify through a separate channel
Contact the sender using a number or address you already have. Do not use the contact
details in the suspicious message. If a manager appears to be asking for gift cards
or a payment change, call them.

## Step 3. Report
Use the report button in your mail client, or forward the message to the security
mailbox as an attachment so the headers are preserved.

## Step 4. If you already clicked
Report it immediately. Speed matters more than embarrassment. Change your password
from a different device and tell IT exactly what you entered and when. Nobody is
disciplined for reporting quickly.

## What happens after you report
The security team reviews the message, checks whether anyone else received it, and
blocks the sender if needed. Reports are used to improve filtering, not to track
individual employees.
